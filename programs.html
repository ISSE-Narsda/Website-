Our Postgraduate Programs

NUC-accredited M.Sc. and Ph.D. programs in space science and engineering

Space Physics

The Space Science program covers a broad range of disciplines, from meteorology and geology, to lunar, solar, and planetary science, to astronomy and astrophysics, to the life sciences.

Duration: 18 months (MSc) | 3 years (PhD)
Learn More →

Geoinformatics and GIS

The Geoinformatics program at ISSE involves teaching, research and hands-on training on the use and appreciation of different tools for different tasks performed from theory to practice.

Duration: 18 months (MSc) | 3 years (PhD)
Learn More →

Aerospace Engineering

The Aerospace Engineering Program at ISSE is concerned with advanced training, research and innovation development of aircraft, spacecraft, and satellite system engineering.

Duration: 18 months (MSc) | 3 years (PhD)
Learn More →

Systems Engineering

Systems engineering enables building, analyzing and managing a system—be it electrical, mechanical, chemical, biological or one involving business processes and logistics.

Duration: 18 months (MSc) | 3 years (PhD)
