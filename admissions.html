Admissions

Take the first step toward a career in space science

M.Sc. Admission Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in a relevant field from a recognized university
  • Minimum CGPA of 3.0 on a 4-point scale or 3.5 on a 5-point scale (Second Class Upper)
  • 18 months duration (12 months coursework + 6 months research leading to an MSc thesis)

Ph.D. Admission Requirements

  • M.Sc. in a relevant field from a recognized university
  • Minimum CGPA of 3.5 on a 4-point scale or 4.0 on a 5-point scale
  • Minimum 3 years duration (1 year coursework + 2 years research)
  • Graduation requires publishing two papers from the thesis in at least a SCOPUS-indexed journal

Application Process

  1. Visit the AUST website to access the application form
  2. Complete the application form with accurate information
  3. Submit all required academic transcripts and documents
  4. Pay the application fee as specified
  5. Submit your application before the deadline
  6. Await admission decision from the admissions committee
