Aerospace Engineering

M.Sc. and Ph.D. Program

Program Overview

The Aerospace Engineering Program at ISSE is concerned with advanced training, research and innovation development of aircraft, spacecraft, and satellite system engineering.

Students gain expertise in aerodynamics, propulsion, structural analysis, and spacecraft design, preparing them for careers in the aviation and space industries.

Course Structure

  • Aerodynamics and Fluid Mechanics
  • Aircraft and Spacecraft Propulsion
  • Structural Analysis and Design
  • Orbital Mechanics and Spacecraft Dynamics
  • Satellite Systems Engineering
  • Avionics and Control Systems
  • Research Methodology
  • MSc/PhD Thesis Research

Program Duration

M.Sc.

18 months
12 months coursework + 6 months research

Ph.D.

3 years
1 year coursework + 2 years research

Admission Requirements

M.Sc. Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Aerospace Engineering or related field
  • Minimum CGPA of 3.0 on 4-point scale or 3.5 on 5-point scale (Second Class Upper)

Ph.D. Requirements

  • M.Sc. in Aerospace Engineering or related field
  • Minimum CGPA of 3.5 on 4-point scale or 4.0 on 5-point scale
  • Publication requirement: two papers from thesis in SCOPUS-indexed journal

Career Prospects

  • Aerospace Engineer
  • Aircraft Design Engineer
  • Spacecraft Systems Engineer
  • Propulsion Specialist
  • Satellite Engineer
  • Academic Researcher
  • University Lecturer
